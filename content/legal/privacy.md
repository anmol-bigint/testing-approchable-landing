# Privacy Policy

Approachable (operated by BIGINT Solutions) · Last updated: 23 September 2026

## 1. Who we are

Approachable ("Approachable", "we", "us", "our") is a brand operated by BIGINT Solutions, a sole proprietorship, with its registered office at Block 15, Flat 805, My Home Vihanga, Gachibowli, Hyderabad, 500032, Telangana, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect your personal data when you visit approachable.dev, register at learn.approachable.dev, join our live AI cohorts, self-paced courses, team trainings, community groups or newsletter (together, the "Services").

For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data. For users in the European Union or United Kingdom, we are the data controller under the GDPR / UK GDPR.

## 2. Personal data we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

- Registration details: full name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, country and city.
- Professional details: job title or role, company or organisation, years of experience, LinkedIn profile URL.
- Learning goals: what you want to build or learn, your use cases, and any other answers you give in our registration or feedback forms.
- Payment details: billing name, email, phone, amount, currency, transaction ID and payment status. Card, UPI, net-banking and wallet details are entered directly on Razorpay's secure checkout. We never see or store your full card number, CVV or banking credentials.
- Tax details (optional): GSTIN and billing address, if you request a GST invoice.
- Course activity: capstone projects, assignments, quiz answers and scores, progress, questions you ask, and messages you send to the mentor.
- Communications: emails, WhatsApp messages, support requests and feedback you send us.
- Testimonials: reviews, quotes, LinkedIn posts or project showcases you choose to share with us.

### 2.2 Information collected during live sessions

Live sessions are held on Microsoft Teams/Zoom and are recorded. If you turn on your camera or microphone, or type in the chat, your name, image, voice and chat messages may appear in the recording. You can take part with your camera off and use a display name of your choice. We tell you when recording starts.

### 2.3 Information collected automatically

- Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, referring page and time spent, collected through server logs and our hosting provider.
- Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 8.

### 2.4 Information from third parties

We may receive payment confirmation from Razorpay, and, if your employer enrols you in a team training, your name, work email and role from your employer.

## 3. How we use your personal data

We use your personal data only for clear, specific purposes:

- Deliver the Services: confirm your seat, create your learning-platform account, send session links, materials and recordings, and review your capstone project.
- Process payments: take payment, issue invoices and receipts, prevent fraud and handle payment disputes.
- Personalise your learning: use your role, experience and goals to tailor examples, group discussions and mentor feedback.
- Run the learning platform: track your progress, score quizzes and show the cohort leaderboard.
- Community: add you to the cohort WhatsApp group and help you connect with other learners.
- Communicate with you: send schedule updates, reminders, support replies and important changes to our terms.
- Marketing (with consent): send our newsletter, AI insights and news about future cohorts and courses. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- Testimonials (with permission): feature your review, LinkedIn post or project on our website or social media only after you've given us permission.
- Improve our Services: analyse feedback and usage to improve the curriculum and website.
- Legal and safety: meet tax, accounting and legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect our rights and the safety of our learners.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

India (DPDP Act): we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you register, pay or subscribe, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law, such as meeting legal obligations and processing data you voluntarily provide for a specified purpose. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 10). Withdrawal doesn't affect processing already carried out, and we may not be able to continue providing the Services without certain data.

EU / UK (GDPR): we rely on performance of a contract (delivering the course you paid for), consent (marketing, testimonials), legitimate interests (improving our Services, preventing fraud, recording sessions so learners can review them) and legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as described below.

We may disclose personal data if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect our rights, users or the public. If Approachable or BIGINT Solutions is merged, acquired or sells its assets, your data may be transferred to the new owner under this Policy.

We're based in India and many of our service providers store data in India, the United States or elsewhere. When your data is transferred outside your country, we take reasonable steps to make sure it's protected, including using providers with appropriate safeguards and, where required for EU/UK users, Standard Contractual Clauses. ## 8. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies needed for it to work (for example, keeping you logged in to the learning platform). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site may not work properly.

## 9. How long we keep your data

- Account, registration and course data: for as long as you're an active learner, you can rejoin and access your history, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
- Payment and invoice records: up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting laws.
- Session recordings: up to 3 months after your cohort ends, then deleted or anonymised, unless we state a different period for your cohort.
- Marketing data: until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.
- Testimonials: until you ask us to remove them.

After these periods, we delete or anonymise your personal data, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data, or update it.
- Erase your personal data when it's no longer needed or you withdraw consent (subject to legal retention requirements, such as payment records).
- Withdraw consent at any time, including for marketing and testimonials.
- Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (India).
- Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

EU/UK users also have the right to data portability, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to their local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email [ranbeer@gmail.com](mailto:ranbeer@gmail.com). We may need to verify your identity. We'll respond within 30 days or any shorter period required by law.

To stop marketing emails, click "unsubscribe" in any email. To leave the WhatsApp community, just exit the group.

## 11. How we protect your data

We use reasonable security practices, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through Razorpay, access controls that limit data to people who need it, and reputable hosting providers.
